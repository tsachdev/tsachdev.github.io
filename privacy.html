Privacy policy

Graduation Ledger gate. Last updated 4 October 2026.

The Graduation Ledger gate is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Tushar Sachdev, and the only Gmail account it accesses is his own. This policy describes what it does with that account's data.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the gate holds one Google permission: Gmail gmail.modify, for the owner's account. It uses it to:

  • read unread messages in the inbox (sender, subject, date and a short preview), when an assistant the owner has connected asks;
  • carry out an action the owner has approved on a private page with his passkey: mark one message read, move one message to the trash, or save one draft reply. Drafts are never sent.

Where data goes

  • Messages read through the gate are passed to the AI assistant the owner has connected and admitted (currently Claude, by Anthropic), so it can suggest what to do with them. The owner chooses and admits every assistant himself, and can revoke any of them.
  • The gate runs on a server the owner controls. Its Gmail token is stored there and nowhere else.
  • No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own email triage.

What it keeps

The gate keeps a log of requests and decisions: the action, the message's Gmail id, the text of any draft reply, who asked, and what the owner decided. It does not store message bodies, senders or subjects; when the owner reviews a request, those are looked up live from Gmail. The log stays on the owner's server.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The gate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the gate's access at any time at myaccount.google.com, under Security, third-party apps and services.
